Report Suspicious Content
Never pay for a job

Do not pay for interviews, appointments, selection, offer letters or recruitment access.

Verify the notification

Check eligibility, dates, fees, qualifications and application rules at the official source.

Protect personal information

Never share OTP, CVV, passwords, UPI PIN or unnecessary identity documents.
