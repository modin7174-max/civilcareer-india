Terms, privacy & safety / Niyamagalu mattu gopyate

Clear information. Careful decisions.

Important: This is an early-stage practical notice, not legal advice. Obtain professional legal review before accepting payments, collecting candidate profiles or operating at commercial scale.

Independent directory

civilcareer.jobs organises factual summaries and links to original vacancy, examination and study-material sources. It is not an employer, recruiter, government body, examination authority or representative of any listed organisation.

No guarantee or responsibility for outcomes

We do not guarantee accuracy, availability, selection, interview, appointment, salary, examination result, visa or employment. Dates and requirements may change. Users must verify every detail in the official notification or employer page before applying.

Job and examination safety

  • Never pay anyone for a job, interview, appointment, offer letter or guaranteed exam selection.
  • Never share OTP, CVV, passwords or unnecessary bank and identity information.
  • Confirm the employer, department, notification number, official domain and application link independently.
  • Report suspected cybercrime through cybercrime.gov.in.

Candidate privacy / Abhyarthi gopyate

Candidates can browse without creating an account. This launch version does not collect résumés, Aadhaar, PAN, bank information or application forms. Hosting providers may process limited technical logs such as IP address, browser and request information for security and service operation.

Community submissions

Submitted links are kept pending for owner review. Submission does not guarantee publication. Do not submit personal documents, private group messages, phone numbers, referral codes, unlawful content or misleading claims.

Study materials and copyright

Only upload materials you created, own or have permission to distribute. A rights holder may request correction or removal through the public GitHub issue link. Free and preview files may be hosted by third parties whose privacy terms also apply.

Future paid materials

Paid access is not enabled at launch. Before enabling payments, publish refund, delivery, pricing, licence and contact terms; keep full paid files private; issue access only after verified payment; and obtain tax and legal advice.

Updates and removal

Listings may be corrected, rejected or removed when expired, disputed, suspicious or unsupported by an original source. These notices may be updated as the platform changes.
